# Brand image sources

All product/lifestyle images and logos used in this preview come from the Project store packs:

1. **ToWebP** — `/cursor/stores/.../media/towebp/` (canonical SKU filenames)
2. **Chat attachments** — `/cursor/stores/.../media/chat-attachments/` (UUID filenames)

Content hashes match between the two packs (attachments are the same photos as ToWebP, including the two logo PNGs). The repo keeps the readable ToWebP filenames under this folder.

Derived UI logos (black knocked out): `nithyam_logo_white.png`, `nithyam_logo_indigo.png`.
